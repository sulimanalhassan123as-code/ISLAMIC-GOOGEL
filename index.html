    About this project

    Islamic Google is a single, quiet gateway: search the Qur'an and authentic narrations, keep your prayers and dhikr in one place, and find trusted channels, apps and institutions without wading through noise.

    On authenticity

    Qur'anic text and translation come from AlQuran Cloud (Tanzil Uthmani text, Sahih International translation). Prayer times, qibla and the Hijri date come from the AlAdhan API. Hadith are quoted only where the collection is named, and every one links to the full collection so you can read it in context. Nothing here replaces a qualified scholar: for rulings on your specific situation, ask a trustworthy local imam or a recognised fatwa body.

    On the directory

    Every link in the directory was tested and returned a live page before being listed. A link appearing here is not an endorsement of every view published by that organisation, only that it is a real, established resource worth knowing.

    Privacy

    No accounts, no tracking, no analytics. Your bookmarks, tasbih count and theme live in this browser only.
